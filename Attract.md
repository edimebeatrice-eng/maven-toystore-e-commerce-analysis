## 1. Attract Dashboard

### Objective
The Attract dashboard focuses on understanding how effectively the Mavin Toy Store attracts visitors to its website. It provides an overview of website traffic and helps identify patterns in customer interest and acquisition.

### What I Analyzed
- Website sessions and overall traffic volume
- Traffic trends over time
- Changes in visitor activity across different periods
- The relationship between website traffic and customer acquisition

### Key Insights
The dashboard provides visibility into how website traffic changes over time and highlights periods of higher and lower visitor activity. This helps the business understand when customer interest is strongest and identify opportunities to improve traffic acquisition.

### Business Impact
Understanding website acquisition is important because increasing traffic creates more opportunities for customers to interact with the store and eventually make purchases. The findings from this dashboard can therefore support decisions around marketing, customer acquisition, and website engagement.

### Recommendation
The business should focus on strategies that increase qualified website traffic, monitor traffic trends consistently, and identify the periods and acquisition sources that generate the strongest visitor engagement.
