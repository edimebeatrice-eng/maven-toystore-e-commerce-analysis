##  Convert Dashboard

### Objective
The Convert dashboard focuses on understanding how effectively website visitors are converted into customers. It uses conversion rate and related performance metrics to evaluate how well the e-commerce platform turns traffic into completed purchases.

### What I Analyzed
- Conversion rate
- Website sessions
- Orders generated from website traffic
- Changes in conversion performance over time
- The relationship between website traffic and completed purchases

### Key Insights
The dashboard helps identify how effectively the business converts website visitors into customers. Comparing traffic levels with conversion performance makes it possible to distinguish between periods of high traffic and periods where visitors were more likely to complete a purchase.

### Business Impact
A high volume of website traffic does not necessarily result in high sales. Monitoring conversion rate helps the business understand whether visitors are successfully moving from browsing to purchasing and highlights opportunities to improve the customer journey.

### Recommendation
The business should continuously monitor conversion rate alongside website traffic, identify periods of weaker conversion performance, and optimize the customer journey to reduce barriers to purchase and increase the percentage of visitors who become customers.
